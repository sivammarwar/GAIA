Why trust the numbers

Transparent by design.

GAIA never tells you what your future will be. It shows you what it could look like — and exactly why. Hover a card for the detail.

01 / ENGINE

A deterministic financial engine does every calculation. The AI explains it — it never invents a number.

01 / ENGINE

Every projection runs through the same open-methodology engine used across GAIA — same formulas whether you're 24 or 64, so nothing is quietly softened for engagement.

02 / SOURCES

Tax and benefit rules are retrieved from the IRS, SEC, CFPB, and Department of Labor — never from model memory.

02 / SOURCES

Rule updates are dated and versioned. If a contribution limit or bracket changes, your plan re-runs automatically with the new figure.

03 / PRIVACY

Your data is encrypted, never sold, and deletable in full — profile, goals, and conversations included.

03 / PRIVACY

Benchmarks are computed on anonymized, aggregated cohorts only. No individual profile is ever visible to another member or advertiser.
